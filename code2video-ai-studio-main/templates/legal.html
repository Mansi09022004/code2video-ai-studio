Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Code2Video AI Studio is a student project. This page explains, in plain language, what we handle and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email, and a hashed password (or your Google account name and email if you sign in with Google).
  • Content you submit: the Python code you paste, your language and level choices, and any questions you ask the doubt assistant.
  • The videos generated for you, plus your ratings, favourites and feedback.

How we use it

Only to run the service: sign you in, generate your videos, show your library and improve the product. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Third-party services

To create a video, your code and text are sent to an AI model provider (Groq) to plan the explanation, and narration text is sent to a text-to-speech service. Google is used if you choose Google sign-in. Please do not paste secrets such as passwords or API keys into your code.

Storage and deletion

Data is kept on our hosting provider while the service runs. This is a free-tier project, so stored videos and accounts may be reset without notice. To ask for your data to be deleted, open an issue on GitHub.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. No tracking or advertising cookies.
